Morphometric Specification

Polished concave tibial depression fringed by long curved retaining setae; holds ~10–15 mg pollen/propolis per leg

Physiological & Biomechanical Mechanism

Pollen moistened with regurgitated nectar is squeezed upward from the pollen press onto the smooth corbicular floor, anchored by a single central seta and curved marginal hairs.

Primary Anatomical Reference

Hodges, D. (1952). The Pollen Loads of the Honeybee.

Active Specimen Profile Apis mellifera — Operaria

Non-reproductive female performing age-polyethic tasks: nursing, comb construction via ventral wax mirrors, nest thermoregulation, guarding, and nectar/pollen foraging.
